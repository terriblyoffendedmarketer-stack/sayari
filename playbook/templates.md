# Template library: meme and design mechanics (company-agnostic)

A template is a reusable **mechanic**, not a design. One mechanic → several design variants → many posts across clients. Industry-specific memes stay in the client's `content/` meme library; the mechanic lives here.
Status: `seed` (spotted, not built) · `validated` (we agreed it fits) · `built` (a wireframe/design exists; link it).
