### P03 · 25% + 25%: creative heavily rewritten
| Element | Ours | Hers | Why hers is clearer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | "25% + 25% = a sanctioned company." | "Two 'clean' 25% owners don't make a clean company." | Plain-language conclusion. Ours was a puzzle that needs the 50% rule to decode. |
| Questionnaire card | Small grey note: "Signed by the supplier. Not checked against anything." | Red rubber stamp: **TAKING THEIR WORD FOR IT** | A visual label says the point instantly; a sentence needs reading. |
| Registry panel | A list in mono: Owner A 25% / blocked, Owner B 25% / blocked, "Together: 50% → the company is blocked too" | Two side-by-side boxes (big 25% + BLOCKED tag) joined by lines into a red result bar: **"Result: 50% blocked. | Extra detail | "Section 4", "Registry record ·" | Removed | Detail labels are noise at 2-second reading. |
| Takeaway | None on the image | Bottom line: "Know who you're doing business with, **not who they say they are**." | The image ends on the moral, so it works without the caption. |
| Colour | Red only in small pills | Red stamp + red result bar | Risk red used boldly to carry meaning (within the design system's `risk` token). |
**Lessons:**
4. **Headline = the conclusion in plain words**, not a riddle or formula. `applied` (kit README, CAPTIONS.md)
5. **Show the logic visually**: stamps, boxes that add up, a result bar. Don't describe it in sentences. `applied` (kit README)
6. **Spell out the consequence for the reader** ("You just onboarded a violation"). `applied`
7. **End the image on a one-line takeaway**, so it stands alone without the caption. `applied`
8. **Cut metadata labels** (section numbers, "registry record ·", dates) unless they carry meaning. `applied`
9. Use risk red **boldly when it carries the meaning**. `testing`

### The distilled rules (now in the rule files)
- **2-second test:** someone with zero context gets the image in 2 seconds and the caption's point in one read.
- **Image:** plain-language headline (the conclusion) · logic shown visually · consequence stated to "you" · a takeaway line at the bottom · no metadata noise.
- **Caption:** single image ~30–90 words; carousel ~60–120 · one idea · max one personality move · facts as bullets · plain CTA · the brand line is one concrete benefit sentence.
