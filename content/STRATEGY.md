# Sayari LinkedIn: 3-month strategy (anchor, not a cage)

Drafted 2026-11-10. Brand page, **3–4 posts a week → about 40–45 posts over 13 weeks.** Review it at the end of each month against results in `content/posts/POSTS.md` and change what isn't working.

## North star
A compliance analyst sees a Sayari post, thinks **"that's literally my job"**, saves or tags someone, and over three months comes to see Sayari as *the brand that gets us*. Then when their team looks at tools, they say the name.

**How we'll know (in order of importance):**
1. **Comments from practitioners** that add their own story ("mine is…", tagging a colleague)
2. **Saves and reposts** (useful enough to keep)
3. Who follows: the share of new followers with analyst/officer titles (not vendors)
4. Later, mentions in sales conversations ("I saw your bingo post")
Impressions matter least; one hundred of the right people beat ten thousand of the wrong ones.
*Needed from Sayari:* current follower count and the last 12 months' top posts, as a baseline.

## The 3-month arc (follows the brief's story: meet them mid-drown → name the enemy → show the shift)

### Month 1 (weeks 1–4): "We see you"
**Job:** earn attention and become recognizable. The reader learns that this account understands their day.
- Mix: the brief's split from day one, **35% pain · 25% relief · 20% receipts · 20% news**. Both posts so far are pain, so the next ones are relief and receipts.
- Themes: false positives all day · the data assembly tax · the swivel chair / Frankenstack · understaffed and accountable · **the name passed, the owner didn't** (validated).
- Ideas: ✅ I01 name match · ✅ I03 bingo · I05 questionnaire vs record · I04 the PURE story carousel · I38 "Did you mean" search · I40 the analyst's calendar · I12 the "quick one" Slack thread · I42 "What tool?" "…a spreadsheet." · I02 every Jose Gomez · I08 demo questions (saveable) · I39 the compliance alignment chart · I06 a score is not a defense · I45 the Moosavi carousel (the brief's own receipts example, if Sayari shares the case).

### Month 2 (weeks 5–8): "It's not you, it's the stack, and the owner"
**Job:** name the real enemy and start **teaching**. The reader learns something they'll use.
- Mix moves to the brief's target: **35% pain · 25% relief · 20% receipts · 20% news.**
- Themes: trust but can't verify (questionnaires stop at tier 1) · "an analyst checked a spreadsheet" isn't an answer · a score is not a defense.
- Ideas: I35 10 compliance shortcuts that backfire · I22 rule-and-penalty carousel (a real enforcement action) · I21 case study as a story (the live sanctioned vendor) · I18 how fraud slips through and the check that catches it · I43 before / during / after the audit · I44 which analyst are you? · I24 graph with the inflection point · I32 the evolution of a sanctions screen · I27 tips to be a better analyst · I30 contrarian takes · I15 Frankenstack (n.) · I11 the breakup text to seven tools.

### Month 3 (weeks 9–13): "Here's a better way, and the receipts"
**Job:** show the shift with proof. The reader starts to associate Sayari with the answer.
- Mix: keep the target, but lean into **receipts and relief**, which is where product proof lives.
- Themes: blind exactly where you're growing · three vendors, sixty workflows · deadline and volume crunch.
- Ideas: I17 six tabs → one query · I41 red-string corkboard → one clean graph · I20 ownership-chain carousel · I19 dashboard screens with striking data · I09 the 72% telecom stat (if approved) · I36 expert quotes (a named Sayari analyst's face) · I31 practitioner screengrabs (UGC) · I33 knowledge graph · I34 the registry-openness map · I07 one address, many companies · I28 AI workflows for compliance teams.
- *Blocked on Sayari:* cleared product screens, approved proof wording, a named analyst willing to front a teardown (the brief wants a face on receipts).

## Weekly rhythm (the default; swap freely)
| Slot | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | **Relatable** (pain mirror) | Starts the week with "that's my job" |
| Wed/Thu | **Teach or prove** (relief / receipts) | The value post; carousels live here |
| Fri | **Save or smile** (lists, quadrants, bingo-style) | Friday 3pm is the brief's moment; easy engagement |
| When news lands | **Headline → what it means for you** (within 24–48h) | Relief instead of fear; only when there's real news |

## Recurring series (formats people learn to recognize)
- **Friday 3pm:** the weekly relatable/fun slot (bingo, calendar, quadrant, playlist).
- **The receipts:** about 2 a month, a real case told as a story carousel (case studies, enforcement actions).
- **Save this:** checklists and lists (demo questions, shortcuts that backfire, first-month truths).
- **Headline → what it means for you:** the newsjack template, as events happen.
A series makes each post easier to make and easier for the audience to recognize.

## Guardrails (always)
Proof gates (no unsourced numbers; client clearance for customer proof) · never name competitors · the beginner-language test · no raw memes for now · personality in every caption (`playbook/CAPTIONS.md`, `content/VOICE.md`).
