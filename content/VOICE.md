# Sayari voice (on top of playbook/CAPTIONS.md)

From the brief, plus what worked in P01–P02.

**We are:** validating (we've sat in the chair) · unflinching (we say the quiet part: a risk score isn't a defense; the enemy is the stack, not you) · human and a little funny (the category is all doom; we bring wit and relief) · proof-backed (every claim shows its work, like the product).

**We sound like:** a senior analyst who's seen it all, talking to a peer at 5:58 pm. Dry, specific, on their side.

**Signature moves so far:** translation jokes ("Translation: that's your problem now.") · comic specificity ("7 Google searches") · deadpan ("It holds. Mostly.") · "It's not you. It's the stack." · the wink P.S.

**Sayari's line in a caption:** one or two plain sentences, always "relief", tied to a USP:
- *source, not score:* "your call comes with its evidence"
- *ownership depth:* "who owns the owner"
- *one place:* "the record, its sources and your decision sit together"

**Never:** "judgment infrastructure", "zero-resistance", "leverage synergies", "single pane of glass", "world model" stats, fear-mongering, naming competitors, mocking the reader.

**Words that land with this ICP (beginner-safe):** potential match, false positive, alert queue/backlog, clear by EOD, audit/examiner, "how did you know?", who owns the owner, questionnaire, the 50% rule (explained), tabs/spreadsheet. **Explain when used:** UBO, L1, SDN, FOCI, UFLPA.
