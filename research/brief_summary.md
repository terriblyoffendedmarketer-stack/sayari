# Sayari brief: the rules in one page

Source: "Sayari: Social Audit & Strategy" PDF (10 pages; the original is in the user's Downloads). Screenshots #01 and #02 in the idea doc are snippets of pages 2 and 10.

## Strategy
- The category (Exiger, Altana, Kharon, Moody's) sells **fear, from authority, to executives**. Nobody talks to the practitioner. Sayari's whitespace: **human, validating, a little funny**, which competitors built on urgency can't follow without contradicting themselves.
- The website's precision ("judgment infrastructure", world-model stats) is **social kryptonite**: same asset, opposite effect. On social you have two seconds.

## Who we talk to
- **Primary:** compliance officers / heads of financial crime, sanctions, KYB (the Protector + the Skeptic: "I see you defending. Manual screening is killing you."); investigations & intelligence analysts (the Optimizer + the Builder: "Better signal. Faster hunting.").
- **Not optimized for:** supply chain leaders, CCO/CRO/CPO budget holders, government mission owners, AI/security/architecture approvers.
- **What they feel:** powerless but accountable, blamed for what they can't see, sceptical any tool is different, quietly terrified of the next audit.

## Voice
Validating · unflinching · human and a little funny · proof-backed. Banned words: see `research/lexicon.md`.

## The arc
Meet them mid-drown → name the real enemy (the Frankenstack, not them) → show the shift (one evidence-based system, the risk they couldn't see, an answer they can defend) → they go from defending to building.

## Pillars
1. **The Frankenstack Reckoning:** the work done by hand is what's drowning them. Provocation: "Your screening process is a spreadsheet, a Google tab, and a prayer."
2. **A Score Is Not a Defense:** when a regulator asks "how did you know?", only original-source evidence answers.
3. **The Risk Hiding in Plain Sight:** the exposure that ends careers is one ownership hop away, not on a list.

## Formats and mix
Pain mirror 35% · Product as relief 25% · Receipts / investigation teardown 20% (the strongest format; a real case with a named analyst's face) · Headline → what it means for you 20% (relief, not fear).

## Channel and cadence
LinkedIn only. Brand page 3–4×/week; ghostwritten exec posts 2–3×/week each; newsjacks within 24–48h.

## Proof points (from the brief; the owner chose to keep them visible; get written approval before posting)
- No. 3 global telecom: 72% of flagged risk was on no watchlist, hidden in nested ownership (60K+ partners screened continuously)
- Fortune 50 energy supermajor: 1.2M records screened in real time vs a competitor's 24–48 hours
- Fortune 50 energy company: 75+ data sources collapsed into one; homegrown system retired
- Top-5 global tech company: chose Sayari over the supply-chain incumbent for sub-tier mapping
