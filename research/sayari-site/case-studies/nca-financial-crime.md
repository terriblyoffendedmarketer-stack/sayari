# NCA: Financial Crime Investigation

Source: https://sayari.com/resources/case-studies/nca-financial-crime/
Published: 2026-04-03
Type: case-study (UK law enforcement; government lane)

## UK Federal Law Enforcement Agency

£10M in hidden criminal assets traced in the first month, including properties purchased without mortgages that existing tools had failed to uncover.
£10M hidden criminal assets identified · 20× investment growth · 40+ police forces

## The challenge
The agency routed open-source intelligence requests through a centralized unit, creating delays for investigators working against strict 30-day regulatory windows. Its legacy corporate data provider (built for credit assessment, six figures a year) lacked trade data, property records, and cross-border connectivity. Investigators were stitching together partial views of complex, cash-intensive businesses, consistently leaving gaps in the evidentiary chain.
- Centralized OSINT request queue vs. strict 30-day case windows
- Legacy provider lacked trade data, property records, cross-border connectivity
- £100K+ annual cost for an inadequate capability

## The solution
Rather than positioning the platform as a replacement, the champion argued the entire request-based investigative model was broken, and embedded "real data or real source" criteria into formal procurement. Sayari Graph was deployed for beneficial ownership resolution, putting data directly in investigators' hands.

## Results
£10M in hidden assets identified in the first month. Investment grew more than 20× from the legacy provider's annual cost, with 56% expansion in under 12 months; coordination across 40+ police forces.

> "Investigators who once waited in a queue for centralized intelligence requests now access data directly, in real time, on live cases." (Deputy Director, Financial Investigator, UK Federal Law Enforcement Agency)
