## Top 5 Global Energy Company

Sixty distinct risk screening workflows unified against a single primary-source data layer.
60 risk screens unified · 12B+ primary-source records · 250+ jurisdictions

## The challenge: scale without infrastructure, and an audit trail that couldn't survive scrutiny
A patchwork of legacy data providers, manual processes and disconnected workflows: each business unit ran its own screening logic against its own data sources. No unified view, no consistent standards. Legacy providers delivered static profiles based on secondary, self-reported information. When officers needed UBO chains in high-risk jurisdictions, SOE connections or military end-user status, the data was not there; analysts stitched partial views together by hand.

## The solution
One connected data platform and workflow engine for all 60 screening workflows, piloted on the hardest populations (SOEs, MEUs, complex UBO chains across China, Russia and the Middle East).

## Results
Sixty workflows on one data layer; every finding traces to an original source document. "When regulators ask for documentation, the team provides evidence-based adjudication, not opaque risk scores."

> "Compliance officers who previously spent the majority of their time assembling data from multiple sources can now focus on analysis and adjudication." (Chief Compliance Officer, Top 5 Global Energy Company)
