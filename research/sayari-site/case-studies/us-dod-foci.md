## Federal Defense Agency

Source-linked FOCI screening for missile defense supply chain risk, selected over alternatives that could not provide original-source evidence.
<10 personnel covering the SCRM mission · 40+ hours of technical validation calls · 0 FOCI illumination data before

## The challenge
The agency's Quality, Safety, and Mission Assurance office screened aerospace and defense suppliers for counterintelligence threats, foreign influence, and solvency risk. Team of fewer than 10 people, responsible for supply chain risk assessments across all programs. Cyber and operational risk had tools; no tool filled the FOCI and sub-tier ownership gap. The mission required source-linked evidence for senior leadership and interagency briefings.

## The solution
Began through a DoD CIO pilot. The champion, a PhD-level SCRM expert, logged 40+ hours of technical calls validating that the API could feed the agency's custom government-owned (GOTS) risk-scoring algorithm. Key differentiator: full data provenance. Government buyers explicitly rejected "black box" tools; competing solutions were rejected specifically because they could not provide source-linked evidence.

## Results
Automated, source-linked FOCI screening integrated into the mission-owned stoplight risk dashboard. Leadership gets briefing-ready intelligence backed by original source documents, not opaque algorithms.

> "Selecting any other source would result in substantial duplication of cost and unacceptable delays." (SCRM Lead, Federal Defense Agency)
