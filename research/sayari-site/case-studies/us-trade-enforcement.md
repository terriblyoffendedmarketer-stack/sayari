## Federal Trade Enforcement Agency

1,600 users across seven field locations on a single primary-source intelligence layer for forced labor, fentanyl, and cartel investigations.

## The challenge: three mission types, one data architecture requirement
Forced labor, trade-based money laundering, fentanyl precursor trafficking, sanctions evasion, and cartel networks all converge at the point of import. Agencies need corporate ownership data combined with trade data, connecting who owns what to who ships what. Data scientists need bulk data, import specialists need investigative UI tools, and everyone needs supply chain mapping. No prior vendor delivered all three.
- $3.7B in UFLPA detentions since June 2022: enforcement scaling fast

## The solution
Built over five years: bulk data (10.6B+ records) for data scientists, Graph UI + supply chain mapping for investigators, API for automated screening. Embedded services: biweekly program meetings, monthly training, a custom agency newsletter, field road shows (LA, Miami, Houston, Chicago, NY, San Juan, HQ).

## Results
1,600+ user accounts, 9,400+ unique logins, 625+ personnel trained across seven field locations. Supports forced labor enforcement, trade compliance, fentanyl precursor investigations, AD/CVD cases, and cartel analysis.

> "When the agency enforcing UFLPA, investigating fentanyl networks, and disrupting cartel finances relies on this data, it validates the platform for every company subject to those same regulatory regimes." (Program Director, Federal Trade Enforcement Agency)
