# Marine P&I Club: KYC/Sanctions

Source: https://sayari.com/resources/case-studies/marine-pi-club-kyc-sanctions/
Published: 2026-04-03
Type: case-study (anonymized)

## World's Largest Marine Insurer

An incumbent platform failed during peak renewal season; it was replaced with a customized compliance system the team controls.
~50% of the global merchant fleet underwritten · 2× annual spend vs. incumbent · 250+ jurisdictions covered

## The challenge: the incumbent failed during the one moment that matters most
P&I clubs must screen counterparties against sanctions spanning Russia, Iran, North Korea and secondary jurisdictions. Vessel ownership is deliberately opaque: beneficial owners hide behind layers of shell companies in flag-of-convenience jurisdictions. And it must be fast: thousands of policies in weeks during renewal season.

Then, in February, the busiest renewal period, the legacy platform went down. Vessels that could not be screened could not be underwritten. Policies could not be issued. Revenue was lost in real time, and the compliance team had no fallback. The outage exposed deeper problems: rigid, non-customizable workflows and data gaps in Southeast Asia, the Middle East and West Africa, exactly where maritime risk concentrates.

## The solution
A structured re-evaluation and a day-long on-site workshop in Norway with a test environment built for their use case; sanctions and KYC teams worked real scenarios. The decisive moment was workflow flexibility: building their own process instead of bending operations to a rigid tool. SLA-backed uptime. (A 2023 evaluation was revisited two years later, once the gaps had closed.)

## Results
Not a cost decision (2× the spend): operational reliability, workflow control, and data quality where maritime risk lives. Zero RFPs required.

> "Workflows match our processes, not the other way around. Data coverage reaches the jurisdictions where maritime risk actually lives." (Head of Compliance, World's Largest Marine Insurer)
