## The challenge: two years of FOCI work, zero enterprise sanctions screening
For energy companies running major parts of US electric and gas infrastructure, a missed sanctions match is not a compliance footnote; it is a national security incident. The company had used an investigative platform for two years, mainly for Foreign Ownership, Control, and Influence (FOCI) analysis, but had no automated, enterprise-wide sanctions screening. With ~75,000 suppliers, every new vendor relationship carried unquantified sanctions risk.
- FOCI only, no enterprise sanctions screening
- Vendor onboarding, supplier management, partner screening all without systematic checks
- ~75,000 suppliers without automated coverage

## The solution: the live detection that proved the difference
A head-to-head test against the company's actual vendor list, versus a well-known legacy provider. The result was decisive: Sayari identified a sanctioned entity on the company's ACTIVE vendor list that the competing solution missed entirely. The screening team had nearly approved a vendor relationship with a sanctioned party. The graph caught it because entity resolution connected the vendor's corporate structure to a designated entity through an ownership chain the legacy system could not trace.

## Results: selected at a 2× price premium, validated by the live detection
Every supplier, partner and vendor is now screened against 10.6B+ source records across 250+ jurisdictions, automatically.

> "When the security leadership briefs the board, it is with confidence that their screening infrastructure is the most rigorous in the industry." (Chief Security Officer, Top-Tier U.S. Energy Infrastructure Holding Company)
