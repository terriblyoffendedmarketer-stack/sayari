## The challenge: three systems, manual reconciliation, and trading desks that can't wait
Entity data, sanctions screening, and adverse media lived in three separate vendor systems. Manual reconciliation across three systems was fundamentally incompatible with trading speed.
- Three-system fragmentation: entity data, sanctions, and adverse media in separate systems
- Trading desk vs. compliance speed: manual reconciliation incompatible with trading velocity
- OFAC 50 Percent Rule exposure: ownership-based restrictions invisible to list screening
- Audit trail fragility: clearance rationale reconstructed from three separate system outputs

## The solution: one API call, a unified counterparty profile with ownership-chain screening
Sayari Graph consolidated 75+ data sources (government registries, sanctions lists, adverse media, trade records) into a single counterparty intelligence layer.
- Three-to-one consolidation: one contract, one audit trail, no reconciliation gaps
- Straight-through processing: unified profiles enable automated clearance for routine transactions
- Continuous ownership monitoring: Signal watches the full counterparty universe for OFAC-relevant changes

## Results
Three vendor contracts replaced by a single Sayari integration. The team is structurally prepared for the expansion of OFAC 50 Percent Rule enforcement and new primary sanctions in emerging energy markets.

> "Counterparty risk programs running on three separate systems have an audit trail problem, not just a workflow problem. Consolidated intelligence, a single timestamped profile with primary-source evidence, is the only documentation that satisfies regulatory examination standards." (Head of Trading Compliance, Fortune 50 Energy Trading Company)
