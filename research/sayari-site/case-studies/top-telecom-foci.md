## #3 U.S. Telecom

Eight hundred critical infrastructure vendors screened for foreign ownership in 90 days, under a regulatory deadline.
~800 critical infrastructure vendors · 500M+ companies in ownership graph · continuous monitoring

## The challenge: "Your vendors passed sanctions screening. Their owners were not checked."
Two regulations converged: the FCC rip-and-replace mandate (screen ~800 critical infrastructure vendors for foreign ownership, control or influence, FOCI) and PADFA (evaluate data-sharing partners for foreign adversary connections). There was no automated screening capability, only manual research and self-attestation.
- Self-attestation cannot reveal foreign government ties hidden in ownership chains

## The solution
Analysts got direct access to ownership chain analysis, beneficial ownership resolution, and foreign government connection identification across 500M+ companies, tracing vendor ownership through multiple jurisdictions to find connections "that self-attestation questionnaires would never reveal." Legal used the same platform for PADFA.

## Results
FCC screen completed and documented; a repeatable PADFA process for every new data-sharing relationship; continuous FOCI monitoring that catches ownership changes and new designations.

> "We can now independently verify vendor ownership claims against authoritative source data, rather than relying on vendor self-reporting." (Head of TPRM, #3 U.S. Telecom)
