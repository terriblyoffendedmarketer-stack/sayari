## Major Global Digital Payments Platform

Entity match rate above 60%, a step-change improvement that displaced the legacy provider as the primary KYB engine.
60%+ entity match rate · 250+ jurisdictions · 10.6B+ source records

## The challenge: three screening platforms, one structural problem they shared
Entity match rates hovered well below 50%: for most businesses submitted, the system could not return a confident match against registry data. Every unresolved entity meant manual research, a bottleneck that scaled linearly with growth. The aggregated, secondary data worked in Western markets but degraded in Asia, Latin America, Africa and the Middle East, exactly where the platform was growing. As it expanded into new markets, the percentage of verifiable entities actually DECREASED.

## The solution: ownership-chain resolution at the point of application
A head-to-head benchmark on the same entity population, measuring resolution by geography and entity type. Direct connections to 250+ official corporate registries produced a match rate above 60%, with the biggest gains in fastest-growth markets.

## Results
Auto-verification up 25 points; three tools became one. Onboarding decisions that took days of manual research now resolve programmatically.

> "Resolving entities that were previously unverifiable eliminated a significant portion of our manual research queue and accelerated merchant onboarding timelines." (Head of Compliance, Major Global Digital Payments Platform)
