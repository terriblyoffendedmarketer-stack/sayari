## The challenge: 75% of compliance time spent assembling data, not analyzing risk
A Fortune 100 integrated operating company (aerospace, industrial automation, building automation, energy) screened third parties with legacy data providers whose coverage was inadequate. Compliance officers spent 75% of their time manually verifying records and 25% on risk analysis. The aggregator could not resolve entities in critical jurisdictions, particularly China, where the company needed to find Military End Users (MEUs) and State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) hidden in opaque UBO structures.
- 75% manual verification / 25% risk analysis
- Legacy provider could not resolve entities in China
- MEUs and SOEs hidden in opaque UBO structures
- Compliance portability needed across spinoff divestitures

## The solution
In a pilot, Sayari returned actionable intelligence for 95% of entities screened, with every finding backed by the original source document, shifting the posture "from opaque risk scoring to transparent, evidence-based adjudication." Deployed as the global data standard for the Due Diligence Center of Excellence.

## Results
600,000+ entities screened against primary-source registry data; the platform was replicated across three independent entities created by divestitures.
