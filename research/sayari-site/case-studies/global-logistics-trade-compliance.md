## The solution

Sayari Graph resolved entity identity by matching shipper-provided names against government registries, corporate filings, and trade data to identify the actual entity behind a customs declaration. The API integrates into the TMS at the point of shipment entry, screening every party (shipper, consignee, notify party, intermediary) against sanctions and restricted party lists while simultaneously resolving ownership chains. ## Building for the future

With party-level screening operational, the company is extending the platform to build transshipment risk scoring, combining party data, route data, and goods classification to identify shipments with diversion indicators. As BIS and OFAC extend enforcement to logistics intermediaries, comprehensive ecosystem screening will become a competitive differentiator.
