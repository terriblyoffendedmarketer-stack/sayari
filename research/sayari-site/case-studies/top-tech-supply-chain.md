## Top 3 Global Tech Company

Supply chain provenance built to the standard of federal trade enforcement, verifying every supplier against authoritative source records.
42% of supply chain leaders extend visibility past tier 1 · 500M+ companies mapped · 10.6B+ authoritative source records

## The challenge: "Your direct suppliers are screened. Their suppliers are not."
The cloud supply chain organization wanted full provenance for data center hardware, at the same level of supplier visibility CBP applies to imports: every component from raw material to final assembly, every entity screened for sanctions, export controls and forced labor. Self-attestation and questionnaires break down beyond tier one.
- McKinsey 2025: 95% have tier-one visibility, only 42% extend to tier 2 or beyond
- Verification must work without depending on suppliers to self-report
- Cloud build-out scaled faster than questionnaire-based mapping could absorb

## The solution: actual trade data, not declarations (4B+ trade transactions)
Integrated via API into the provenance system: entity identity, ownership and relationships across 500M+ companies in 250+ jurisdictions, connecting supplier self-reported data to independently verified registry records. The graph surfaces shared ownership between a tier-one supplier and a sanctioned entity, SOE ownership links, and structure changes that signal jurisdictional risk shifts.

## Results
Every component carries a verified provenance chain; every supplier is continuously monitored.

> "We now build data centers with supply chain visibility that exceeds the capability of most government agencies." (VP Supply Chain Provenance, Top 3 Global Tech Company)
